Please read before you give

  1. Nature: The "support" described on this page is a voluntary gift to me personally. It is not a charitable donation.
  2. Recipient and taxes: The recipient is me personally (Guangyi Zhao), not a 501(c)(3) or any other tax-exempt organization. Your gift is not tax-deductible, and I report the amounts I receive as income under US tax law.
  3. No consideration: A gift comes with no goods, services, rights, or promise of any return. All content here is free whether or not it receives support; giving or not giving does not affect your access to anything.
  4. Refunds: Once completed, gifts are non-refundable in principle. Billing errors and similar cases are handled under the rules of the payment platform (Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors).
  5. No investment relationship: A gift does not create an investment, loan, partnership, or employment relationship, and gives rise to no equity, debt, or other rights.
  6. Platform terms: For payments made through third-party platforms, the handling of funds and the protection of your personal information are governed by that platform's terms of service and privacy policy.
