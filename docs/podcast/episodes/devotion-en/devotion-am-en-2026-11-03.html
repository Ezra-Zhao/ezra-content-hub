[AM] Do Not Be Weary in Doing Good

Good morning. Today is Tuesday, November 3rd. This is Daily Devotion. On a Tuesday morning, the seed sown yesterday shows no sign of movement yet: the right thing done drew no applause, the person you helped sent no word back, and the thing you have held on to for so long seems to be standing still. Do not rush to pass judgment on today just yet. First hear a farmer's word: the harvest has its own season.

Today's verse is Galatians chapter 6, verse 9: "Let us not be weary in doing good, for we will reap in due season, if we don't give up."

The words that pierce deepest are "in due season." A seed buried in the soil shows nothing for the first few days, yet the farmer does not dig it up every day to check — he knows the growing happens where eyes cannot see. Doing good is the same: the gentle word you added for your family, the extra share of work you quietly carried, the help you gave in secret — at the time there may be no echo at all, and you may even be misunderstood. Paul did not say doing good brings an instant harvest. He said: do not be weary, do not give up, and in due season you will reap. The season is in God's hands, not ours; what is in our hands is today's one shovelful of soil. What today asks of you is simple: keep sowing the seed that should be sown, keep watering what should be watered, and do not abandon the field just because nothing has sprouted yet. The ground does not betray the one who keeps tilling it, and God will not forget the good you have done in secret.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, we confess that we often lose heart when we cannot see results, and we are tempted to lay the good work down. Remind us that the season of harvest is in Your hands. Teach us to sow in peace today and wait with patience, not weary and not giving up. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord establish the work of your hands. See you tomorrow.
