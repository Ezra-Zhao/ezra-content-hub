[PM] Romans 3:28: Justified by Faith

Good evening. Today is Thursday, November 19th. This is Daily Devotion. A day has passed, and if a voice of self-blame is still in the heart, it is mostly over something not done well today, turned over one by one again at night. Tonight, first hear this settled word of Paul, put the foundation you stand on firm, and then sleep in peace.

Tonight's verse is Romans chapter 3, verse 28. The Scripture says: We maintain therefore that a man is justified by faith apart from the works of the law.

D. Martyn Lloyd-Jones, speaking of justification, said that the low spirits of many believers do not come from not trying hard enough, but from a foundation that is not set right: always thinking that by doing a little better today and changing a little more tomorrow, one can stand firm before God. If the foundation is wrong, however tall the building rises, the heart has no peace. Paul says a man is justified by faith. Righteousness is not earned; it is given to us by God in Christ: He made Christ bear our sins, and counts His righteousness to our account. Since justification is a declaration made once and forever, tonight do not turn back and stare at old things in self-blame. The prison door is already open; do not keep sitting in the cell. Lay today's shortcomings honestly before the Lord, and trust that He has already dealt with them. His acceptance does not change with the success or failure of this one day. Sleep quietly. Tomorrow, rise not by last night's self-blame to make yourself anew, but on this firm foundation, and follow the Lord again.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You for justifying us by faith, so that we do not stand by our own performance. Tonight we lay today's shortcomings before You and blame ourselves no more. Keep us as we sleep in peace, and keep those we love as well. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
