[AM] One Day in Your Courts Is Better Than a Thousand

逐字稿全文

Good morning. Today is Sunday, November 8th. This is Daily Devotion. Today is the Lord's Day. For six days of the week we rush in and out of all kinds of places; today is the day we come back into His courts. Some people treat going to church as a duty to fulfill, but the psalmist says it is a gain — one day in His courts is better than a thousand elsewhere.

Today's verse is Psalm 84, verse 10. The psalmist says: For a day in your courts is better than a thousand. I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.

The way the psalmist does this accounting is unusual: he counts not by the number of days, but by where you are. A thousand days elsewhere are not worth one day in His courts; even standing at the door as a doorkeeper is better than being an honored guest in the tents of the wicked. Why? Because in His courts there is His presence, there is His word, and there is a people worshiping together. When a person draws near to Him, the heart is set right again: the priorities that the week has pulled out of shape slowly come back into place in worship. When you go to worship today, do not go with a picky heart; go with the psalmist's heart — even standing at the door is better than anywhere else. When you get home you will find that what has been filled is not your schedule, but yourself.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for preparing for us this day of worship. Make us glad to come into Your courts today, simply to draw near to You, to receive strength from You, and then to go back into the life of this week. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord let you enjoy His favor in His courts. See you tomorrow.
