[PM] John 6:11: He Gave Thanks, and Distributed to Those Sitting Down

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Sunday, November 15th. The Lord's Day has come to the evening; during the day we heard the word and our hearts received comfort, and our bodies are also a little tired. Before sleeping, think back on this day: how the Lord provided for us — and so, at rest, place ourselves back into His hands.

Tonight's verse is John chapter 6, verse 11. The Scripture says: Jesus took the loaves; and having given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to those who were sitting down; likewise also of the fish as much as they desired.

John Piper, speaking of this all-providing King, said that five loaves and two fish look like far too little in human eyes, yet in the Lord's hands they were enough to feed five thousand, because He is not only a King who works miracles; He Himself is the provision. The Lord first gave thanks, and then distributed to the crowd, reminding us: provision does not depend on how much we have in our hands, but on whose hands it is placed into. Tonight, count the grace received this day: a meal, a comforting word, a hymn sung in the gathering — all were distributed to us by the Lord's own hand. And for the worries not yet handed over, also learn from the Lord: first give thanks, then entrust them, believing that what we need tomorrow He will still distribute on time. Sleep with thanksgiving, and the heart will no longer calculate its own lack, but rest only in His abundance.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You for Your provision today; You have fed us, and You have also made our hearts rest. Tonight we place tomorrow into Your hands; please keep us and let us sleep in peace. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
