[PM] Luke 8:15: Hold the Word Tightly in an Honest and Good Heart

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Saturday, November 14th. The week has come to tonight; some of the words heard and the Scriptures read during the day have already been forgotten, and some still remain in the heart. Before sleeping, do not hurry first to sleep; quietly think for a moment: this week, where did God's word land in my heart?

Tonight's verse is Luke chapter 8, verse 15. The Scripture says: Those in the good ground, these are those who with an honest and good heart, having heard the word, hold it tightly, and produce fruit with perseverance.

John Sung, speaking on the soil of the heart in the sowing, said that there is no problem with the seed; the problem is the soil. When the word falls by the roadside, it is easily hardened by busyness and noise; when it falls on rocky ground, with sins hidden and undealt with at the bottom of the heart, it retreats as soon as hardship comes; when it falls among thorns, it is choked by worries and affairs and cannot grow. Good soil is not soil that never had rocks and thorns, but soil willing to let the Lord clear it: to speak plainly before Him about the things that are hidden, to lay down the affairs that crowd the heart, and to hold the word tightly with an honest and good heart. Bearing fruit also takes perseverance — not seeing results the same night one hears, but taking root in quietness. Tonight, prepare the soil of your heart just this way: confess one hidden thing to the Lord, lay down one worry that entangles your heart, keep well one sentence you heard today, and wait with perseverance for it to bear fruit in your life.

Let's pray together. Lord, clear the soil of our hearts tonight, remove the rocks and the thorns, and make Your word take root in an honest and good heart and bear fruit with perseverance. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
