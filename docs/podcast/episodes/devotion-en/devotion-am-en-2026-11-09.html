[AM] Malachi 3:6: He Does Not Change

Good morning. Today is Monday, November 9th. This is Daily Devotion. A new week has just begun, and things are already changing: a plan you made can be overturned by one phone call; your body's condition, news from home, arrangements at work — none of it is in our hands. Early on a Monday morning, first hear a word that does not change, and let your heart have a steady footing first.

Today's verse is Malachi chapter 3, verse 6. God says: For I, Yahweh, don't change; therefore you, sons of Jacob, are not consumed.

Preaching on this verse, Spurgeon said that everything around us changes, but He alone does not change: His nature does not change, His love and faithfulness do not change, and the promises He has spoken do not change. When a person reads a promise and no longer finds it sweet, often it is not the promise that has changed; it is his own heart, stirred up by other things. The rock has not moved; the one who moves is the person standing on the rock, shaken by the wind. So he urged people not to rest on the promise only with the tips of their toes, as if testing it, but to lean their whole weight on it, steadily, and let His word hold up today. Jacob went through many trials in his life — fleeing, being deceived, losing loved ones — yet he was not consumed, not because he himself stood firm, but because the God who loved him remained the same throughout. Going out today is the same: plans may change and arrangements may shift; first place this week in the hands of the unchanging God, and then handle, one by one, the things that need doing.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, a new week has come, and we admit that many things are not in our control. Thank You that You do not change, that Your promises stand firm, and that Your love endures. Help us today to rest securely on Your word and to walk steadily the road we should walk. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord give you, in a week of changes, a peace in your heart that does not change. See you tomorrow.
