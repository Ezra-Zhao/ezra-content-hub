[PM] Psalm 42:5: Hope in God

一句话要点：诗篇42:5：应当仰望神——诗篇 42:5 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解改编自钟马田《属灵低沉总论》、经彻底重写，祷告为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Tuesday, November 10th. The day has come to tonight; perhaps everything got done, yet the heart still feels heavy: a certain sentence keeps turning in the mind, and a certain situation to face tomorrow is still being rehearsed in the heart after lying down. Tonight before sleep, hear how the psalmist speaks to his own soul.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 42, verse 5. The Scripture says: Why are you in despair, my soul? Why are you disturbed within me? Hope in God! For I shall still praise him for the saving help of his presence.

Preaching on this verse, Lloyd-Jones said that most of a person's unhappiness comes from always listening to his own heart speak, instead of speaking to his own heart. As soon as the heart opens its mouth, it is yesterday's hardships, today's wrongs, tomorrow's worries, one sentence after another. He also reminded us that when the body is tired, the heart sinks more easily, and in low seasons we should not be too quick to condemn ourselves. The psalmist did not let it go on speaking; he turned around and asked it: why are you in despair? What reason is there to be disturbed? Then he reminded himself, one by one, of who God is and how many times God had helped him. Tonight we can practice the same way: do not debate with despair all night long; bring it before God, and remind yourself — He helps me with His smiling face; I shall still praise Him. If reminding yourself once is not enough, remind yourself again, until the heart slowly grows quiet. Despair is not tonight's ending; hope is.

Let's pray together. Lord, my soul is sometimes in despair and disturbed. Please make me hope in You tonight, remember the help of Your smiling face, and fall asleep peacefully in praise. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
