[PM] Psalm 100:4: Enter His Gates with Thanksgiving

一句话要点：诗篇100:4：以感谢进入祂的门——诗篇 100:4 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解改编自比尔·约翰逊《神的同在》、经彻底重写，祷告为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Wednesday, November 11th. After a busy day, the body is tired, and the heart still holds things from the day that were not finished. Before sleeping, do not rush to think about tomorrow; bring your heart before God, and end this day with thanksgiving tonight.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 100, verse 4. The Scripture says: Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise. Give thanks to him, and bless his name.

Bill Johnson, speaking on God's presence, said that God does not rest on what people build; He rests on people. His presence is not a program, nor an atmosphere; it is that He is willing to dwell among His people. The psalmist says: enter His gates with thanksgiving, enter His courts with praise; thanksgiving is a road that draws us near to Him. Tonight, before lying down, slowly count: which thing today did He help you get through? Which person did He place beside you? Counting like this, the heart comes out of the day's turmoil and rests in His presence. The things not finished today, lay them down tonight and entrust them to Him again tomorrow; He is with you tonight, and tomorrow He will surely walk with you too. Tonight you can rest your heart and entrust yourself into His hands.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You for being with us today. Let us rest tonight in thanksgiving and praise, and wake tomorrow still walking in Your presence. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
