[PM] He Instructs Me in the Night

一句话要点：诗篇16:7：祂在夜间也指教我——诗篇 16:7 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Saturday, November 7th. After a busy day, the body has stopped, but the mind is still turning: the words spoken a little too heavily today, the matter still not dealt with — once we lie down, they come up one by one. Tonight before sleep, hear something David said about the night — it turns out the night is not only for sleeping; it is also for being reminded.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 16, verse 7. The Scripture says: I will bless Yahweh, who has given me counsel. Yes, my heart instructs me in the night seasons.

In the daytime there are too many voices: the phone, the messages, people coming and going, and His counsel is often drowned out. When night falls and everything quiets down, the heart wakes up instead — David says his heart instructs him in the night: this thing was not done right, that word was said too heavily, and that kindness for which he forgot to give thanks during the day. Such instruction is not condemnation; it is a gentle reminder, like a father giving you a light pat before bed: child, hand these things over to Him; do not carry them into your dreams. Tonight, be quiet for one minute as well, and let your heart run through the day once: the wrongs that need confessing, confess them before Him; the kindnesses that deserve thanks, thank Him in your heart; what needs to be let go, let it go. Then bless the God who instructs us even in the night, and fall asleep in peace. One who has been instructed by Him in the night wakes the next day with a light heart and steady steps.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You that You instruct us in the day and remind us in the night as well. Tonight, quiet our hearts; whatever needs to be entrusted, we place it all in Your hands, and we sleep in peace under Your watch. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
