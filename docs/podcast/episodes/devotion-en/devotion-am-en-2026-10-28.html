[AM] All Things Work Together for Good

Good morning. Today is Wednesday, October 28th. This is Daily Devotion. Open the window, and may the first breeze of the morning bring you quietness.

Today's verse is Romans chapter 8, verse 28: "We know that all things work together for good for those who love God, to those who are called according to his purpose."

When the verse says "all things work together," it does not mean that everything we meet is itself a good thing. A schedule suddenly thrown off, an email that gets on your nerves, even a dull ache somewhere in your body — none of these is pleasant in itself. But God's hand is like that of a skilled weaver. If you look at the back of a tapestry, all you see is a tangle of rough threads and dark, dead knots; it can even look a little ugly. But when the weaver turns it over, those deep black and gray threads are exactly what set off the brightness of the gold and red ones, and together they form a beautiful picture. As you head out today, whether what lands in your hands is a smooth day or a problem that has you stuck, don't rush to discouragement. See it as one thread in God's hand: He is patiently weaving all of it into good for your life.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for governing our whole day. Give us a settled heart, so that when we cannot make sense of the trouble in front of us, we still firmly believe You have made no mistake. Whether today holds smooth going or rough patches, we entrust it all into Your mighty hand. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord give you His special peace and assurance today. See you tomorrow.
