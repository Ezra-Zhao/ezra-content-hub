[PM] Ephesians 2:6: Raised Up With Him, Seated With Him in the Heavenly Places

一句话要点：以弗所书2:6：与基督一同坐在天上——以弗所书 2:6 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解改编自倪柝声《坐、行、站》、经彻底重写，祷告为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Friday, November 13th. The week has come to tonight; the body is already tired, yet the heart still cannot stop: the things not done well today, the people to be faced tomorrow — lying down, they still pass through the mind one by one. Before sleeping, do not hurry first to think how to make up for them; hear an order Paul speaks of in Ephesians.

Tonight's verse is Ephesians chapter 2, verse 6. The Scripture says: And raised us up with him, and made us to sit with him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus.

Watchman Nee, speaking on sit, walk, stand, said that the Christian life does not begin with walking, but with sitting. Christ accomplished the work of cleansing sins, and then sat at the right hand of the Most High; God has also made us sit with Christ in the heavenly places. To sit is to put the whole weight of the body onto the chair — to unload the burden, ourselves, and the future, all onto the Lord. Seated firmly, a person no longer holds himself up by his own effort. Adam was created on the sixth day, and his first day was the Sabbath: God worked first, man entered rest first, and only then worked. Tonight the order is the same: there is no need first to put yourself in order until you are good enough, and only then dare to come before Him; the work of redemption He has already accomplished — tonight first sit down and receive, unload this whole week's burdens onto Him, and rest peacefully in the work He has finished. The road to walk tomorrow, and the battle to stand in, both begin from tonight's rest.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You that You have accomplished everything. Make us tonight lay down our own striving, sit together with You, and sleep peacefully in Your finished work. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
