[PM] He Loves You in Silence

一句话要点：西番雅书3:17：祂默然爱你——西番雅书 3:17 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Thursday, October 29th. Dim the lights a little, and let a heart that has been rushing about all day slowly settle and grow quiet.

Tonight, before sleep, let's read a verse: Zephaniah chapter 3, verse 17: "Yahweh, your God, is in the midst of you, a mighty one who will save. He will rejoice over you with joy. He will rest in his love. He will rejoice over you with singing."

There is an especially tender phrase in this verse: "He will rest in his love" — He loves you in silence. During the day, our ears are filled with hurrying messages, unavoidable friction, and calculations and anxieties that will not stop turning in our minds. But at night, when the noise falls away, God does not come with severity to tally up how much you finished today, or whether you did well enough. He is simply beside your bed, quietly watching over you and loving you. This love does not make a noise and does not keep score; like the gentle night, it wraps around you. However tired today has left you, however weak you may feel, you do not have to keep holding yourself up any longer. Tonight, leave everything that has no answer in the darkness. Know that you are deeply loved, then close your eyes with a settled heart and sleep well.

Let's pray together. Dear Heavenly Father, thank You for keeping watch over me in the stillness of the night with a love that is silent yet steadfast. I place all of this day's worries and weariness into Your hands, and I will not hold on to them any longer. Calm the turmoil in my heart, and let me sink into sleep and find rest again in Your quiet love. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
