[AM] He Will Supply Every Need

Good morning. Today is Thursday, November 5th. This is Daily Devotion. On a Thursday morning, before you even step out the door, the bills may already be weighing on your heart: this month's payments, what you owe, what the children need — and a few things still have no source at all. The word not enough presses down first thing in the morning, and the heart panics before the day begins. First hear a very steady sentence Paul wrote from prison.

Today's verse is Philippians chapter 4, verse 19. Paul says: My God will supply every need of yours according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus.

Notice: the man who wrote this was not standing in a warehouse; he was sitting in a prison. Paul himself was in need, yet he dared to say will supply — because he was not counting his own stock, but God's riches. Listen carefully to the measure in this verse: God supplies according to his riches in glory — He gives by His abundance, not by the size of our lack. And what He promises is every need of yours, not every want: what we need, He takes responsibility for; what we merely want can wait. The Philippians had first sent their little to support Paul, and Paul writes back: what you gave away will not make you poor, for behind you stands a rich God. It is the same today. Do not start the morning staring at the gap until your heart panics. Lay today's real needs, one by one, before Him. If He supplies according to His riches, today's portion will not be missing.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, we confess that we see the lack first and panic first. Thank You for Your promise to supply every need of ours according to Your riches in glory. Teach us today to entrust our needs to You first, and then calmly do what is ours to do. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord supply every need of yours and give your heart a sense of enough. See you tomorrow.
