Good morning. Today is Wednesday, November 11th. This is Daily Devotion. The week has reached its middle; our strength seems half spent, yet the work is not finished: a hard phone call to make, a hard decision to take, family inside and out needing care. Before going out, first hear a sentence Paul wrote in prison.

Today's verse is Philippians chapter 4, verse 13. Paul said: I can do all things through Christ, who strengthens me.

Liu Tong, speaking on the secret of overcoming our circumstances, reminded us that this sentence is not a slogan. Before Paul said it, he said he had already learned to be content: sometimes in plenty, sometimes in lack; he had known both fullness and hunger. He was not shouting from a comfortable place; he had learned it in every kind of circumstance. Victory is not waiting for circumstances to improve; it is that, when circumstances have not changed, the heart is first not dragged along by them. It is the same today: how hard things are is not in our hands, but we can choose in whose hands our focus rests. Move your eyes away from what is lacking, and count how He has helped us up to now; then, by the strength He adds, do well the one thing right in front of us. Strength often comes one step at a time; having come to this step today, His help will not stop here either.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for helping us up to now. Teach us to learn contentment, and by the strength You add, to do well, one by one, the things we should do today. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord let you today, through Him, walk every step steadily. See you tomorrow.
