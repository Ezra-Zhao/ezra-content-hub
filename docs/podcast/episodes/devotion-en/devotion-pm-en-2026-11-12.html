[PM] Psalm 23:4: You Are With Me in the Valley

一句话要点：诗篇23:4：祢与我同在就不怕——诗篇 23:4 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解改编自刘彤《你可以不再惧怕》、经彻底重写，祷告为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Thursday, November 12th. The day has come to tonight, and the things met in the daytime still remain in the heart: worrying news, work not yet finished; when the night grows quiet, fear becomes clearer instead. Before sleeping, return first to Psalm 23, and hear what David said in the valley.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 23, verse 4. David said: Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me. Your rod and your staff, they comfort me.

Liu Tong, speaking on no longer being afraid, pointed out that earlier in Psalm 23 it says He leads me, He makes me lie down; but when it comes to the valley, the address suddenly changes: it is no longer a distant He, but a face-to-face you are with me. The valley was not taken away, but the companionship drew nearer. The shepherd's rod drives away wild beasts, and his staff brings back the sheep that stray; David was not afraid, not because the valley was not dark, but because the Shepherd was right beside him. He also said that faith is not a feeling, but a choice: the heart still has fear, yet we still choose to place ourselves in the Shepherd's hands. It is the same tonight: tomorrow's road is not yet clear, but tonight first remember: you do not walk alone; He is with you, and His rod and His staff comfort you.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You that You are still with us in the valley. Take the fear from our hearts tonight, and let us rest in peace in Your companionship. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
