[AM] Numbers 14:9: Yahweh Is with Us — Do Not Fear

Good morning. Today is Wednesday, November 18th. This is Daily Devotion. Before we step out the door, the first voice we easily hear is the voice of difficulty: how big the matter is, how hard the people are, how far the road is. Listen to that voice long enough, and the heart steps back before the feet have even started. Today the Lord's word reminds us: hear the whole report, but look even more carefully at where God is.

Today's verse is Numbers chapter 14, verse 9. The Scripture says: Only don't rebel against Yahweh, neither fear the people of the land; for they are bread for us: their defense is removed from over them, and Yahweh is with us: don't fear them.

Bill Johnson, speaking of this history, said that the report of the ten spies was factual in every sentence: the cities were great, and the people were strong. But their report had no God in it. Caleb and Joshua looked at the same land and said a different sentence: Yahweh is with us; do not fear. The voice of unbelief often does not stand out; it only makes the problem very big and God very small. Whichever report a person listens to, that is the road the person will walk. The first generation listened to fear and wandered in the wilderness; Caleb had a different spirit, followed the Lord fully, and later entered the promised land. Today you too will hear many reports. Do not step back too quickly. Lay the facts before God and ask: Lord, how do You want me to trust You here? Make the preparations you should make with care, and take the step you should take with courage. A promise is not given for us to watch from the side; it is given for us to hold fast and move forward.

Let's pray together. Lord, keep us from listening to the voice of fear; let us listen only to Your word. Whatever great thing we face today, help us remember that You are with us, and go out with courage. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord give you courage for the road today. See you tomorrow.
