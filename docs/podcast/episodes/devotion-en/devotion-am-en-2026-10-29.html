[AM] The Lord Is My Shepherd

逐字稿全文

Good morning. Today is Thursday, October 29th. This is Daily Devotion. May your heart rest in the peace that comes from God in the early light of this morning.

Today's verse is Psalm 23, verse 1: "Yahweh is my shepherd; I shall lack nothing."

Sheep grazing in a pasture never actually know where the next patch of grass is, and they have no idea how to work out whether there will be enough grass for tomorrow. The reason a sheep can lower its head and graze in peace, and lie down at ease, is not that it has mastered a map of the road ahead, but that walking in front of it is a shepherd who knows every road — and who knows the sheep. For many of us, the moment we wake in the morning, our hearts are gripped tight by income that has not yet landed, bills not yet paid, and all the uncertainties of tomorrow. We strain to work out every day ahead of us in full, thinking that only when we have calculated everything through will we feel safe — and the more we think, the more worn out we become. But the verse reminds us: whether you lack or not is decided not by how much you have stored up, but by whom you are following. A sheep does not need to understand the shepherd's whole plan; it only needs to stay close behind him. Lay down tomorrow's worries. For this step of yours today, He has already made full provision.

Let's pray together. Lord, facing the pressures of life and the unknown of tomorrow, we often feel that our strength is not enough. Forgive our small faith, our constant urge to take control of everything ourselves, and give us a heart that simply looks to You. Today we place the work in our hands and the needs of our home into Your gracious hand. Amen.

May the loving Shepherd watch over your steps all day today and supply everything you need. See you tomorrow.
