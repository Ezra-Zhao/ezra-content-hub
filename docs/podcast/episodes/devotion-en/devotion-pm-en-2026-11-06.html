[PM] A Very Present Help

一句话要点：诗篇46:1：祂是随时的帮助——诗篇 46:1 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Friday, November 6th. At the end of the week, the body still carries a load it has not set down: things that went wrong, news still hanging in the air. The body is tired, but the heart will not stop. Before you sleep tonight, hear one short, steady sentence. It does not argue; it simply gives an address — where help is found in trouble.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 46, verse 1: God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

There are three gifts in this one verse, each closer than the last: He is our refuge — there is a place to hide. He is our strength — when our own strength is gone, He gives. And He is a very present help — not help by appointment, and you do not have to wait until things get worse before you may ask. The words very present are exactly what tonight needs: no waiting for tomorrow, no tidying up your feelings first; you may hide in Him this very moment. When Psalm 46 was written, armies were moving outside the city, yet the psalmist could say, we will not fear — not because the turmoil had stopped, but because the hiding place had been found. Tonight, leave the hanging matters, one by one, at the door of the refuge; do not carry them into your sleep yourself. He who is a very present help is awake through this night too.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You that You are our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Tonight we place in Your hands the tiredness of this week and the things still hanging. Let us lie down in safety in You. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
