[AM] Renewed Strength for Those Who Wait

Good morning. Today is Saturday, October 31st. This is Daily Devotion. After a whole busy week, on this Saturday morning your body may have stopped, yet your heart may still be hanging in midair, already tired over next week's matters.

Today's verse is Isaiah chapter 40, verse 31: "But those who wait for Yahweh will renew their strength. They will mount up with wings like eagles. They will run, and not be weary. They will walk, and not faint."

When the prophet spoke these words, his hearers were trapped in a foreign land, with no end to the road in sight. God did not give them a timetable first; He gave them one word first — wait. Waiting is not doing nothing, idling the hours away. Waiting is taking your eyes off your own strength and fixing them on the God who never grows weary. An eagle mounts up on its wings not by flapping desperately, but by entrusting its wings to the rising current of air. Today is Saturday; do not rush to spend next week's strength in advance. Hand over, one by one, the work left unfinished and the questions still unsolved, and wait for Him in the quiet. You will find that strength is not squeezed out by gritting your teeth; it is regained in the waiting.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for reminding us on this Saturday morning that You never grow weary and never slumber. Forgive us for always running ahead of You, using up our own strength and filling our hearts with anxiety. Lord, teach us to wait for You, to entrust our wings to Your wind, and to renew our strength in You. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord keep you resting in peace today, and renew your strength. See you tomorrow.
