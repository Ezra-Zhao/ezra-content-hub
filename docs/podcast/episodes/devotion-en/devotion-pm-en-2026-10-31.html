[PM] In Peace I Will Sleep

一句话要点：诗篇4:8：安然躺下睡觉——诗篇 4:8 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Saturday, October 31st. This week's matters come to a pause tonight. Put your phone a little farther away, let the room grow quiet, and rest for a while in this stillness.

Tonight, before sleep, let's read a verse: Psalm chapter 4, verse 8: "In peace I will both lay myself down and sleep, for you alone, Yahweh, make me live in safety."

When David wrote this psalm, he was surrounded by slander and hostility; by rights he should have tossed and turned all night. Yet he said, "In peace I will both lay myself down and sleep." His peace did not come because all his troubles were solved; it came because he had settled one thing in his mind: the one keeping watch through the night was not him — it was God. Before falling asleep, we love to run the day over once more in our heads, and to shoulder tomorrow's burdens before we even get into bed. Tonight you can do the reckoning David did: in the hours when you are asleep, the world goes on turning just the same, and whether the doors at home are locked or not matters less than one solid fact — Yahweh alone makes you live in safety. Since the One watching over you is Him, you can leave the unfinished questions with today, place yourself in His hands, and sleep deeply.

Let's pray together. Lord, this week's running about ends here. Thank You for keeping watch over us Yourself tonight, and for letting us live in safety. Take away the worries we still clutch tightly as we lie down. Let us fall asleep like children in Your care. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
