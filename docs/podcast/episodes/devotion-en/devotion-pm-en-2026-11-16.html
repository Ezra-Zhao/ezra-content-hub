[PM] Psalm 34:8: Taste and See That Yahweh Is Good

一句话要点：诗篇34:8：尝尝主恩，便知道祂是美善——诗篇 34:8 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解改编自陶恕《神的良善》、经彻底重写，祷告为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Monday, November 16th. Monday has come to the evening; many things were done, and the heart is tired too. Before sleeping, do not only count today's hardships; count also the grace of the Lord you tasted today. When grace has been tasted, the heart knows rest.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 34, verse 8. The Scripture says: Oh taste and see that Yahweh is good. Blessed is the man who takes refuge in him.

A. W. Tozer, speaking of the goodness of God, said that goodness is God's heart: He delights to show grace and is set on being kind to people. No one has ever received anything from God because they deserved it. That we were created, that we are preserved, that our sins are forgiven, that our prayers are answered — all of this comes from His goodness. The psalmist did not tell us only to listen to others say that the Lord is good; he told us to taste it ourselves. Tonight, look back on this day: a hard matter that did not crush you, a timely word of comfort, your family coming home safely, an evening meal eaten in peace — none of these is something to take for granted; all of them are the taste of the Lord's grace. Having tasted, take refuge in Him, and do not carry tomorrow's burdens to bed. Entrust the unfinished work, lay down the outcomes you worry about, trust this good Lord, and lie down in peace.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You for Your goodness today; we have tasted the flavor of Your grace. Tonight we take refuge in You. Please keep us as we lie down in peace, and keep those we love as well. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
