Good morning. Today is Saturday, November 14th. This is Daily Devotion. On a weekend morning, a person wants to rest, yet one question often stays in the heart: I have believed in the Lord for these years — has my life truly been renewed? Many outward things have been done, but the temper, the fears, and the old habits inside are still there. Before going out, first hear a sentence the Lord Jesus spoke to Nicodemus.

Today's verse is John chapter 3, verse 3. Jesus answered: Most certainly, I tell you, unless one is born anew, he can't see God's Kingdom.

Wang Mingdao, speaking on the true meaning of being born again, said that being born again is not patching up the old life, nor an outward renewal of baptism, membership, or rituals; it is the Holy Spirit, through God's word, giving birth to a new life within a person. Nicodemus was a religious leader with morals and learning, yet the Lord still told him he must be born anew, because the question is not how bad a person is, but where his life comes from. A person who is born again will surely show it: first, his attitude toward sin changes — he no longer lives at ease in sin, but hates sin; then he loves God's word, loves righteousness, and loves the brothers and sisters. Today, do not only ask how long you have believed; ask whether this new life is growing in daily life: when temptation comes, do you follow the old nature, or turn back by the new life? If you find you have fallen, do not give up on yourself either — that a born-again person hates sin is itself proof the new life is still at work within.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for causing us to be born again through the Holy Spirit and Your word. Renew our hearts today, make us hate sin and love righteousness, and live out the likeness of the new life. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord make you walk today by the new life. See you tomorrow.
