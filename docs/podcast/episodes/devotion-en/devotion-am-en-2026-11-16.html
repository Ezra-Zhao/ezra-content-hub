[AM] 1 John 1:9: If We Confess Our Sins, He Forgives and Cleanses Us

Good morning. Today is Monday, November 16th. This is Daily Devotion. A new week has begun. If your heart is still carrying things from last week that were not dealt with cleanly, you will have no strength to move forward. Some words were never made clear, and some things have been kept hidden, and the longer they are hidden, the heavier they become. Today, first come before the Lord, set your heart right, and then go out.

Today's verse is 1 John chapter 1, verse 9. The Scripture says: If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us the sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

A. W. Tozer, speaking of the justice of God, said that justice is not an outward rule laid on top of God; justice is what God is. When He forgives those who confess, it is not soft-hearted leniency: the cross has already changed our position. Christ suffered for sin, and so justice now stands on the side of those who take refuge in Him, declaring forgiveness and cleansing us from unrighteousness. So confessing is not a risky test of whether God will be angry; it is returning before His unchanging nature and speaking our true condition aloud. Today, do not simply say a general "Lord, forgive me" and move on. Confess one concrete thing: a wrong word spoken to your family, a bitterness hidden in your heart, a promise you did not keep. When the confession is concrete, the cleansing is real too. Begin this week with a clean heart, and your steps will be light and your work upright.

Let's pray together. Lord, we confess our sins before You. Please, according to Your faithfulness and righteousness, forgive us and cleanse us from all unrighteousness, so that we enter this new week with a clean heart. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord make you walk in the light today. See you tomorrow.
