[AM] He Will Make Your Paths Straight

Good morning. Today is Monday, November 2nd. This is Daily Devotion. On a Monday morning, the road of a new week has just opened up: deadlines to chase, people to meet, things still unsettled — they all crowd into the heart at once. Do not rush to work out every step in your head just yet. Today, first hear an old word: hand the road over, and you will walk it steadily.

Today's verses are Proverbs chapter 3, verses 5 and 6: "Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and don't lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight."

The hard part of this word is "with all your heart." It is not that we do not trust; it is that we trust halfway and calculate the other half — saying we surrender it, while our hearts still clutch a backup plan, as if God had not heard clearly, arranging every detail all over again. Proverbs says, do not lean on your own understanding. That does not mean switching off your mind; it means not letting that bit of cleverness sit in the master's seat. And "acknowledge him" does not mean waiting for instructions to fall from the sky. It means, in each concrete matter, acknowledging Him as Lord first: before signing this paper, before walking out this door, before answering this letter, ask first — how does He see this? That is how a path becomes straight: not by seeing it all clearly overnight, but by walking one step, acknowledging one step, and letting Him guide one step. This Monday, take the thing you are gripping tightest, loosen your hand first, hand it over — then set out.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, a new week lies before us, and we confess that we often lean only on our own bit of cleverness, our hearts both anxious and tired. Teach us to trust in You with all our hearts, and to acknowledge You in all our ways. You have promised to make our paths straight, so we will follow in peace. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord guide your steps this week. See you tomorrow.
