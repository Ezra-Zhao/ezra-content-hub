[AM] 2 Chronicles 20:15: The Battle Is Not Yours, But God's

Good morning. Today is Thursday, November 12th. This is Daily Devotion. Some mornings, before we have even gone out, the heart has already met trouble: the matter is bigger than expected, the strength we can use is small, and even the next step is not clear. Before going out, first hear a sentence Jehoshaphat heard in a desperate place.

Today's verse is 2 Chronicles chapter 20, verse 15. The Scripture says: Don't be afraid, and don't be dismayed because of this great multitude; for the battle is not yours, but God's.

Zhuo Erjun, speaking on God's work in a desperate place, said that when Jehoshaphat heard the allied army pressing in, the first thing he did was not to arrange his troops, but to admit that he had no power to resist and did not know what to do, and to fix his eyes only on God. Admitting our powerlessness is not giving up; it is handing the battlefield back to God. God's word came: you shall not need to fight; only take your position, stand still, and watch Him bring salvation. Later, the singers went ahead of the army, and before they had seen any sign of victory, they began to praise; the place of suffering became the Valley of Blessing. Today, if there is one thing too big for you to carry, do not first force yourself to hold on: honestly admit your limits, stand firm in your place, and leave the outcome in God's hands. The battle is not yours, but God's; you only need to stand still and watch how He works.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, facing things beyond our strength, we admit that we are limited. Keep us from fear, make us stand firm, fix our eyes only on You, and leave the battle in Your hands. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord make you stand firm today and not be afraid. See you tomorrow.
