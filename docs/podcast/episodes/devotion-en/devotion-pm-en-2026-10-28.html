[PM] Goodness and Mercy Follow Me

一句话要点：诗篇23:6：恩惠慈爱随着我——诗篇 23:6 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Wednesday, October 28th. Night has deepened. Set aside for now the unfinished odds and ends in your hands, and let shoulders that have been tense all day slowly relax.

Tonight, before sleep, let's read a verse: Psalm 23, verse 6: "Surely goodness and loving kindness shall follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in Yahweh's house forever."

The psalmist uses a very tender word here: "follow." In its original setting, it does not mean trailing along far behind; it is like two loyal guards staying close at your back, never a step away. Reaching Wednesday night, perhaps your steps have grown heavy, and your heart is still holding the tasks you could not finish today, the conversation that did not come together, or some small thing that makes you sigh. We are used to looking ahead, worrying whether we will have the strength for tomorrow's road, and we rarely look back before sleep. In truth, that breeze that brushed past you at the crossing today, that moment of quiet that crossed your tired heart — those were footprints quietly left by goodness and loving kindness. He has not let you walk through a single evening alone. They have escorted you all the way, until you came through your door, set down your load, and lay down in bed. Tonight, you can sleep soundly.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for watching over me all day long. When I was busy rushing about and even overlooking You, Your goodness and loving kindness, like faithful companions, escorted me all the way to my door. Now I place every unfinished worry into Your hands, and I sleep in peace in Your house. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
