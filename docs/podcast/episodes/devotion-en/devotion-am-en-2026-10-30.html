[AM] Seek First His Kingdom

Good morning. Today is Friday, October 30th. This is Daily Devotion. As the week draws to a close, whatever gains and losses or busyness your heart may be turning over, may you begin this day in God's peace.

Today's verse is Matthew chapter 6, verse 33: "But seek first God's Kingdom, and his righteousness; and all these things will be given to you as well."

On a Friday, the desk is often piled with loose ends not yet sorted out, or a proposal that has to be handed in next week. It is so easy to put "all these things" — the visible results, the income, the evaluations — in the very front row of our hearts, until we are driven along, worn out to the bone. The Lord Jesus asks us to notice that word "first." To seek God's Kingdom first does not mean laying down the work in our hands; it means putting the order of our hearts back in its right place. Today, as you answer each email and handle each conversation, watch whether you are striving out of anxiety, or living out God's honesty, kindness, and uprightness. When you are no longer held hostage by a sense of lack, but hand the rule back to your heavenly Father, your steps grow calm. You attend to aligning yourself with God's heart; your Father will attend to supplying all that you need.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, on this morning at the close of the week, we place the tension in our hearts into Your hands. Forgive us for so often getting things backwards, growing flustered and uneasy as we chase after the gains and losses right in front of us. Lord, steady our gaze. Help us honor You in every small thing today, and rest in Your abundant faithfulness. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord keep you in full peace today, and may you experience His provision and His presence in all that you do. See you tomorrow.
