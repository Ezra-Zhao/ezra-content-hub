[PM] Psalm 27:4: One Thing I Have Asked — To Dwell in Yahweh's House and See His Beauty

Good evening. Today is Tuesday, November 17th. This is Daily Devotion. After a whole day, the heart is full of many things, some finished and some still hard to let go of. Before sleep, gather your heart into one thing. Stop letting your thoughts run here and there, and come quietly before the Lord.

Tonight's verse is Psalm chapter 27, verse 4. The Scripture says: One thing I have asked of Yahweh, that I will seek after: that I may dwell in Yahweh's house all the days of my life, to see Yahweh's beauty, and to inquire in his temple.

A. W. Tozer, speaking of the perfection of God, said that all of God's goodness gathered together is what Scripture calls the beauty of Yahweh. David did not ask for many things; he asked for only this one thing: to dwell in the house of the Lord all his life, and to see His beauty. The more clearly a person sees God, the steadier the heart becomes, because He has no lack and He does not change. The things that made us uneasy during the day still come knocking one by one at night. Tonight, do not chase them one by one again. First turn your eyes back to the Lord: think of how He kept you today, and how He did not forsake you. As you see His beauty, the unrest in your heart will slowly settle. Leave tomorrow's questions to ask in His temple, sleep tonight's sleep in peace, and trust this perfect, unchanging Lord.

Let's pray together. Lord, tonight we ask for only this one thing: to dwell before You and to see Your beauty. Quiet our hearts, keep us as we sleep in peace, and keep those we love as well. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
