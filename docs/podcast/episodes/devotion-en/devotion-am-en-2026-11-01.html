[AM] Glad to Go to the House of the Lord

逐字稿全文

Good morning. Today is Sunday, November 1st. This is Daily Devotion. A new month begins on a Sunday morning. Do not rush to turn to next month's bills and schedules just yet; today, first do something more important — draw near to God.

Today's verse is Psalm chapter 122, verse 1: "I was glad when they said to me, 'Let's go to Yahweh's house!'"

When David said this, what made him glad was not a building; it was that someone invited him to draw near to God together. Notice that word — "Let's," we together. Faith was never a road to walk alone with your head down. When someone says to you, "Let's go together," a weary heart gets pulled up by the hand. We do not yet know what this month will bring. But if, on its first day, we first turn our steps toward Him, our hearts find their order: worship first, then livelihood; drawing near first, then the rush. Today, if someone invites you to worship together, do not plead tiredness. And if no one invites you, you can be the one who speaks up — send one sentence to family or a friend: let's go together. Your one invitation may be the very thing that lifts someone out of a low place.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for placing a new month in our hands, and for inviting us into Your presence on this Sunday morning. Take away the weariness and the excuses in our hearts, and let us, like David, rise gladly the moment we hear the invitation. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord give you a peaceful start to this month. See you tomorrow.
