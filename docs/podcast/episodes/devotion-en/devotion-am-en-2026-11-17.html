Good morning. Today is Tuesday, November 17th. This is Daily Devotion. As soon as we step out the door, our hearts easily split in two: warm toward people we like, cold toward people who are hard to get along with; careful with things that go smoothly, putting off the troublesome ones. Today the Lord's word gathers our hearts back and calls us to learn the perfection of our heavenly Father.

Today's verse is Matthew chapter 5, verse 48. The Scripture says: Therefore you shall be perfect, just as your Father in heaven is perfect.

A. W. Tozer, speaking of the perfection of God, said that God's perfection is not a goodness that is only a little higher than someone else's. In God there is no degree, no lack, and nothing extra; His holiness, goodness, righteousness, mercy, and love are all full, with nothing discounted anywhere. Just before Jesus said this, He spoke of the heavenly Father who makes His sun rise on the good and on the evil, and sends rain on the righteous and on the unrighteous. The Father's love does not play favorites, and it is not given only to those who deserve it. So perfection, for us, does not first mean doing everything without a single mistake; it means no longer loving only halfway: loving only those who are kind to us, doing only what benefits us. Today, practice one whole thing: say one prayer for a person you do not like, greet someone you usually avoid, and finish to the end a small thing you promised someone. When your heart learns wholeness from the heavenly Father, your steps will be straight.

Let's pray together. Lord, make our hearts like Your heart, showing no favoritism and not giving up halfway. Whoever we meet today and whatever we do, let us live out Your full love. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord give you a whole heart for the road today. See you tomorrow.
