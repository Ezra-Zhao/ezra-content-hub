[PM] Kept in Perfect Peace

一句话要点：以赛亚书26:3：坚心倚赖的，必得十分平安——以赛亚书 26:3 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Wednesday, November 4th. Lying down at night, the body stops, but the heart often does not: the day's events replay in the mind again and again, and tomorrow's worries come knocking early. The more you think, the wider awake you are, and peace feels far away. Tonight, first hear a short word from Isaiah — short, but steady.

Tonight's verse is Isaiah chapter 26, verse 3: You will keep whoever's mind is steadfast in perfect peace, because he trusts in you.

Hidden in this verse is an order: it is not that peace comes first and trust follows; the mind leans steadfastly on Him first, and peace is kept afterward. In the original language, the word peace is spoken twice over, like a double portion — perfect peace. And the one who keeps that peace is God, not ourselves. Our part is to bring the wandering heart back before Him, again and again; His part is to guard this heart, so it does not roam all night. Tonight you do not have to figure everything out first. Hand over, just as they are, the things you cannot untangle; and when they float back up, hand them over again, without scolding yourself. Where the heart leans, there the night rests.

Let's pray together. Lord, at the end of this day, there are still many things our hearts have not set down. Teach us to trust in You steadfastly, and not to chase peace by our own reckoning. You have promised to keep in perfect peace those who trust in You, so tonight keep our hearts Yourself, and let us sleep in peace in You. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
