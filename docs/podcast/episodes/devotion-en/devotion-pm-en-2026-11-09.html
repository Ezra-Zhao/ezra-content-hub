[PM] Psalm 103:8: Abundant in Loving Kindness

一句话要点：诗篇103:8：祂有丰盛的慈爱——诗篇 103:8 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解改编自陶恕《神的怜悯》、经彻底重写，祷告为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Monday, November 9th. The first day of the week has come to tonight: some things got done, and some were done badly — a word spoken too sharply, a task left dragging unfinished — and lying down, the heart still carries a little guilt. Tonight before sleep, first hear how David describes the God before whom we come.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 103, verse 8. The Scripture says: Yahweh is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness.

Speaking of God's mercy, Tozer said that mercy is not a mood God has only now and then; mercy is what God is: when His goodness meets our trouble and our failures, it becomes mercy. So this mercy cannot be used up, and it is not more today and less tomorrow. This verse in the psalm also says He is slow to anger and abundant in loving kindness; and a little further on it says He has not dealt with us according to our sins. That means: coming before Him tonight, we do not first have to tidy ourselves up until we look presentable before we dare to speak; what needs confessing, confess it honestly before Him, and what needs entrusting, entrust it with a quiet heart. Just as He bent down to hear the groaning of the Israelites in Egypt, so tonight He bends down to hear our prayers. Fall asleep with this assurance: what receives us is not how we performed today, but His abundant loving kindness.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You that You are merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness. Tonight we place this day's gains and losses before You; let us fall asleep in peace in Your love. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
