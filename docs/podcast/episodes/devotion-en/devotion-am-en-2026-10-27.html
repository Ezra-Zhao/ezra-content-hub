[AM] Thoughts of Peace

Good morning. Today is Tuesday, October 27th. This is Daily Devotion. May the morning sunlight be with you as we begin a new day in quietness.

Today's verse is Jeremiah chapter 29, verse 11: "For I know the thoughts that I think toward you," says Yahweh, "thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you hope and a future."

We often print this verse on greeting cards, but don't forget: when God spoke these words, His people were far from home, exiled in a foreign land, in Babylon. God did not promise that the hardship would end tomorrow; what He promised was that His heart toward them had never changed. Perhaps you, too, are in an in-between season today: just moved into a new place, with unopened boxes still piled at your feet; started a new job, feeling out of place among unfamiliar faces; or still waiting for an answer that is slow to come. In these wearing gaps, we always worry that our days are being wasted. But remember: even in Babylon, God was not asleep. His thoughts are not to hold you down; in soil that looks like a standstill, He is quietly burying hope for your future.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, when we are in the middle of change and waiting, our hearts often hang in midair. Hold us up with Your thoughts of peace, so that we no longer panic in unfamiliar surroundings, and help us entrust all of today's worries and steps into Your gracious hand. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the peace the Lord gives accompany your every step today. See you tomorrow.
