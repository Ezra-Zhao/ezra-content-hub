[PM] When I Am Afraid, I Will Trust in You

Good evening. Today is Tuesday, November 3rd. This is Daily Devotion. The composure we held together all day sometimes comes apart at night when everything quiets down: the things there was no time to think about during the day surface one by one, and the heart feels a quiet dread. Tonight there is no need to pretend you are not afraid. First hear a word David spoke in the middle of his fear.

Tonight's verses are Psalm chapter 56, verses 3 and 4. David says: "When I am afraid, I will put my trust in you. In God, I praise his word. In God, I put my trust. I will not be afraid. What can flesh do to me?"

Notice that David did not say, "I am not afraid." He said, "When I am afraid" — fear comes, and there is no shame in that; even David was afraid. The difference lies in where a person turns when fear comes: turning the worst outcome over and over in the mind, or turning, like David, to trust in God. The ground of his trust was God's word: what God has spoken will not fall to the ground, so David could go on to say, "I will not be afraid." Tonight, before you sleep, tell Him the very thing that makes you dread, exactly as it is — no polishing, and no need to have the answer worked out first. Even if the fear is still there, the One you lean on is awake all night. What flesh can do is limited; the hands that hold you are not.

Let's pray together. Lord, tonight we confess that there is fear in our hearts, and the worries we pushed down during the day have floated back up. In David's words, when we are afraid, we will put our trust in You, and praise Your faithful word. Settle our hearts, and let us sleep well in Your arms. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
