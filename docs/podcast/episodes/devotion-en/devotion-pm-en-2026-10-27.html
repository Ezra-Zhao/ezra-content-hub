[PM] Perfect Peace

一句话要点：以赛亚书26:3：十分平安——以赛亚书 26:3 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Tuesday, October 27th. Put down for a while whatever is still unfinished in your hands, and let your body and heart, after a whole day of rushing about, slowly grow quiet in this moment.

Tonight, before sleep, let's read a verse: Isaiah chapter 26, verse 3: "You will keep whoever's mind is steadfast in perfect peace, because he trusts in you."

The "perfect peace" in this verse, in the original Hebrew, actually says the word "peace" twice in a row. It does not mean a life with no storms in it; it means a deep, settled security. During the day today, perhaps you felt anxious over messages you could not finish answering, unsettled by arrangements still hanging unresolved, your whole heart like a leaf shaking in the wind. But remember: the steadiness God gives was never built on how smoothly things went; it is built on His faithfulness, which never changes. A steadfast mind is not gritting your teeth and holding on; it is pulling up the anchor of your heart from the drifting affairs of the day, and casting it again into His embrace. It is dark now. All of today's right and wrong, gain and loss — hand them over to Him. You have done your best. Now you can let go.

Let's pray together. Lord, night has come, and we place the day's tiredness, worries, and unease into Your hands. Quiet the noise still churning in our hearts, and give us Your perfect peace. Tonight, may our hearts be anchored deep in You, and may we sleep in rest. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
