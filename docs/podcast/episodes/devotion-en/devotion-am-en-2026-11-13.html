Good morning. Today is Friday, November 13th. This is Daily Devotion. The week has come to Friday: what should have been handed in is not yet finished, the news we are waiting for has not yet come, and the heart cannot help feeling anxious: we have prayed for a long time, and heaven seems to have given no reply. Before going out, first hear a sentence the Lord Jesus left His disciples as He was about to depart.

Today's verse is John chapter 16, verse 24. The Lord said: Until now, you have asked nothing in my name. Ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be made full.

Bill Johnson, speaking on praying from God's heart, said that prayer is not simply reporting our own will to heaven and being done with it; being born again joins our heart with the Father's heart, so we first draw near to Him and understand His heart, and then ask according to His heart — such prayer will surely be answered, and answered prayer brings overflowing joy. He also reminded us that when the disciples could not drive out the demon, they did not make up a set of explanations for the failure, but took Jesus aside and asked why. For prayers not yet received, likewise do not settle for the way things are, and do not give up easily: quiet down and ask the Father what needs adjusting — is it waiting, is it forgiveness, or is it first drawing near to Him? Today, before going out, first spend a few minutes drawing near to Him, then in His name lay out the things on your heart one by one, clearly: ask, and you will receive, and your joy can be made full.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, thank You for joining our hearts with Yours. Teach us to pray according to Your heart, to ask in the Lord's name and not give up easily, and to receive the fullness of joy in Your answer. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord make your prayer answered today, and your joy full. See you tomorrow.
