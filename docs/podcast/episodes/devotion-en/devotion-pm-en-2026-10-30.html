[PM] He Goes Before You

一句话要点：申命记31:8：祂必不撇下你——申命记 31:8 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Friday, October 30th. After a whole busy week, take off your shoes now, let your tense shoulders slowly loosen, and rest for a while in this quiet.

Tonight, before sleep, let's read a verse: Deuteronomy chapter 31, verse 8: "Yahweh himself is who goes before you. He will be with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you. Don't be afraid. Don't be discouraged."

These were the words Moses spoke softly to young Joshua before stepping aside. Ahead of him lay the rushing Jordan and a wilderness he did not know, and anyone's heart would tremble at that. Tonight you are probably much the same: you have just closed this week's laptop or ledger, yet your heart has already quietly taken up next Monday's meeting, the health of your family, or some problem still unresolved. The three most comforting words in this verse are "goes before you." You are not the one who must push open the door of the unknown alone; God has already gone ahead of you. Every morning you will step into next week, every person you will meet, every difficulty you will face — He has already been there first, and He is waiting there for you. Since the One who goes before you is ahead, tonight you can lay down the burden of being the guide, hand tomorrow back to Him, and sleep deeply.

Let's pray together. Lord, this week's running about ends here. Thank You for always walking one step ahead of me, and for laying peace over a tomorrow I have not yet reached. Take away the worry about the unknown at the bottom of my heart. Tonight, let me lie down and rest like a child in the arms of Your presence. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
