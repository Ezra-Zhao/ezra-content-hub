[AM] Be Strong and Courageous

Good morning. Today is Wednesday, November 4th. This is Daily Devotion. On a Wednesday morning, what lies ahead may be a road you have never walked: a task you have never done, a conversation you dread starting, a decision waiting for you to make it. A trembling heart does not mean you are not up to it — standing at the Jordan River, Joshua's heart trembled too. First hear what God said to him.

Today's verse is Joshua chapter 1, verse 9. God says: "Haven't I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don't be afraid. Don't be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go."

Notice that this command does not rest on Joshua himself. God did not say, "You are strong, so do not be afraid." He said, "I am with you." Being strong and courageous is not about storing up enough courage before you set out; it is knowing who walks with you, and daring to take the first step. Joshua was taking up Moses' burden: the river was not yet crossed and the cities not yet faced — anyone would have been afraid and dismayed. God did not deny those feelings; He simply set a greater fact above them: wherever you go, He is there. It is the same today. That thing that makes your heart tremble — you do not have to wait until you stop being afraid to do it. Go out the door with these words, "I am with you": the fear may still be there, but your feet can move first. Very often, courage is not the absence of fear; it is taking one step forward while you are still afraid.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, facing roads we have never walked, we confess that we are afraid and dismayed. Thank You that You do not ask us to become strong first, but promise to be with us. Make us strong and courageous today, and help us take the step we are meant to take. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord go with you and steady your steps. See you tomorrow.
