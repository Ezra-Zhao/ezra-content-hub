[AM] Hear His Loving Kindness in the Morning

逐字稿全文

Good morning. Today is Friday, November 6th. This is Daily Devotion. On a Friday morning, the tiredness of a whole week has piled up, and today still holds a few hard tasks: which one first, which road to take — the heart has no clear answer yet. Before you start arranging the day in your head, hear David's morning prayer. The first thing he asked for was not strength; it was to hear.

Today's verse is Psalm 143, verse 8. David prays: Cause me to hear your loving kindness in the morning, for I trust in you. Cause me to know the way in which I should walk, for I lift up my soul to you.

Notice the order of David's asking: first, to hear His loving kindness in the morning; then, to know the way in which he should walk. The road is not found by rushing out first; it is heard first, then walked. There are two reasons in this verse: for I trust in you, and for I lift up my soul to you. Trusting and lifting up the soul are the posture of hearing. Settle it in your heart first: His loving kindness is already here this morning; it does not wait until the tasks are done. Then lay today's road before Him step by step and ask: this one first or later? This word today, or not today? It is the same for us. Before you go out, be quiet for two minutes and place the most tangled matters before Him. The whole road may not become clear at once, but the step you should take, He will cause you to know.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, in the morning we come to You first. Cause us to hear Your loving kindness, for we trust in You. Cause us to know the way we should walk today, for we lift up our souls to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord let you hear His loving kindness in the morning, and give your steps a road. See you tomorrow.
