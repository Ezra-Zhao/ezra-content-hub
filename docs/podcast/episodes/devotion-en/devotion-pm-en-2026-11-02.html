[PM] Come to Me, and I Will Give You Rest

一句话要点：马太福音11:28：到我这里来，得安息——马太福音 11:28 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Monday, November 2nd. The first working day of a new week, and the burden on your shoulders feels a little heavier than yesterday. Tonight, do not rush to review today's gains and losses. Set the burden down for a while, and listen to a word the Lord Himself spoke.

Tonight's verse, before sleep, is Matthew chapter 11, verse 28. Jesus says: "Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest."

In this word, the Lord does not first ask what you are carrying, how heavy it is, or whether you brought it on yourself. He only says: Come. Labor is the work in your hands that never ends; the heavy burden is the matter in your heart you cannot set down. Today you may have both: the work unfinished, and the heart unreleased. The rest the Lord gives is not something you receive only after the burden is gone; you receive it as soon as you come into His presence. The burden can wait until tomorrow; tonight, first lean against Him and rest. There is only one road to rest, and it is "come to me": no need to tidy yourself up first, no need to wait until your feelings settle — just come as you are, with all your weariness. Tonight, before sleep, hand over one by one the things left undone today, the things that went wrong, the things you are still worrying about — and then sleep in peace. The hands that hold your burden will not loosen all night long.

Let's pray together. Lord Jesus, we have labored all day, and the burden still weighs on our hearts. Tonight we come to You, just as You said, and we ask You to give us rest. The unfinished things and the things we cannot set down — we place them all in Your hands. Watch over us through this night, and let us sleep in peace. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
