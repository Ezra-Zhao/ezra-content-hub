[PM] I Have Overcome the World

一句话要点：约翰福音16:33：在祂里面有平安，祂已经胜了世界——约翰福音 16:33 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Thursday, November 5th. The day did not go too smoothly: some things were left undone, some burdens have no words, and once you lie down and the daytime effort relaxes, the worries float up one by one. Before you sleep tonight, hear one sentence Jesus spoke to His disciples on the last night before the cross — He never pretended there would be no trouble ahead.

Tonight's verse is John chapter 16, verse 33. Jesus says: I have told you these things, that in me you may have peace. In the world you have trouble; but cheer up! I have overcome the world.

In this verse Jesus says two things together, without hiding either: in the world you have trouble — that is true; but cheer up — that is also true. Peace has an address, and it is not found in the world, for the world cannot give it; peace is in Him. And the ground of our cheer is not that trouble will pass us by, but that He has already overcome the world: the cross and the empty tomb are already behind Him; the hardest battle has already been fought — by Him. Tonight there is no need to rehearse the unsolved problems in your mind one more time; rehearsing finds no answers, it only rehearses away your sleep. Place those few things, just as they are, before Him and tell Him: Lord, this one I still have no way through. Then let the rest of the night be kept by the One who has overcome the world.

Let's pray together. Lord Jesus, thank You that You never lied to us: You said there would be trouble in the world, and You also said You have overcome the world. Tonight we place in Your hands the things left undone and the worries we cannot put down. Keep us in Your peace, and let us sleep in safety. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
