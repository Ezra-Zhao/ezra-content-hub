[PM] Peace Will Guard Your Heart

一句话要点：腓立比书4:6-7：一无挂虑，平安守心——腓立比书 4:6-7 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Sunday, November 1st. The Lord's Day has passed, and tomorrow a new week begins again. Tonight, do not rush to line up tomorrow's tasks in your head; let your heart grow quiet first.

Tonight, before sleep, let's read two verses: Philippians chapter 4, verses 6 and 7: "In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus."

When Paul wrote these words, he was still in prison. His "in nothing be anxious" does not mean pretending nothing is wrong; it means giving anxiety an exit: prayer. Anxiety carries tomorrow's matters through tonight in advance. Prayer takes that same matter and places it, with your own hands, before God — and adds a word of thanksgiving. That word "guard" in the verse carries the sense of a sentry standing watch. Tonight, after you lie down, peace will stand watch in your place, guarding your heart and your thoughts. So take tomorrow's list — the people to meet, the bills to pay, the letters still unanswered — and tell Him each one. When you have told Him, set it down. Tonight the one on watch is not you. You may simply sleep in peace.

Let's pray together. Lord, we place tomorrow and the coming week in Your hands. Thank You for promising that peace which surpasses understanding, and for guarding our hearts Yourself tonight. Take away the anxieties we still toss and turn with as we lie down, and let us fall asleep in peace under Your watch. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
