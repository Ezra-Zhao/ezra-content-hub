[AM] Mark 1:3: Make Ready the Way of the Lord, Make His Paths Straight

Good morning. Today is Sunday, November 15th. This is Daily Devotion. Today is the Lord's Day. Before going out to worship, first prepare the road in your heart. After a busy week, there are crooked places in the heart, and places that are blocked. The words John the Baptist cried out in the wilderness are spoken to us today as well.

Today's verse is Mark chapter 1, verse 3. The Scripture says: The voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Make ready the way of the Lord! Make his paths straight!'

Wang Guoxian, reading the Gospel of Mark, said that this Gospel does not begin with the genealogy of Jesus, but first tells of John the Baptist preparing the way, because this Lord came to be a servant; before He drew near to people, He first sent someone to prepare their hearts. Preparing is not excitement made for people to see; it is a real turning in the wilderness: leveling the high places of pride, straightening what is hidden and crooked, and moving away the stones that keep the Lord from coming in. John wore clothing of camel's hair and ate locusts and wild honey; he himself first lived out simplicity and single-heartedness, and only then could he cry out these words. Today, preparing the way of the Lord can begin with small, concrete things: before going to worship, first confess one wrong to the Lord, speak one word of reconciliation to your family, and lay down a resentment stored up in your heart. When the road is made straight, the Lord's word no longer has to go around when it comes in, and we will truly receive help as we hear the word and worship.

Let's pray together. Lord, today is Your day. Please straighten the road in our hearts, remove what is crooked and what blocks the way, and make us worship You and hear Your word with a clean heart. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord renew you today in His house. See you tomorrow.
