[PM] Romans 8:38-39: Nothing Can Separate Us from God's Love

Good evening. Today is Wednesday, November 18th. This is Daily Devotion. A day has passed, and the heart keeps a few things it cannot put down: what was not done well, what we fear may go wrong tomorrow, and an accusing voice we cannot explain, making us wonder whether God has drawn far away. Before sleep, first hear Paul's words of deep persuasion, and put your heart back into God's love.

Tonight's verse is Romans chapter 8, verses 38 to 39. The Scripture says: For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers, nor height, nor depth, nor any other created thing will be able to separate us from God's love which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

R. C. Sproul, speaking of this passage, said that Paul's persuasion did not rest on feeling. If the Father did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up for us all, He will not leave us halfway. A person's feelings change: today we feel near, tomorrow we feel far. God's love does not move with our feelings; it is fixed on Christ. Hardship, distress, the things of tomorrow, the things to come: Paul counted them one by one, and at the end he said, none of them can separate us. Tonight, stop picking petals one by one, guessing whether He loves you or not. Lay today's shortcomings beneath the cross, leave tomorrow's matters in His hand, trust this Lord who never changes, and lie down in peace.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You that Your love cannot be cut off by anything. Tonight we rest our hearts in Christ. Keep us as we sleep in peace, and keep those we love as well. In Jesus Christ's name, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
