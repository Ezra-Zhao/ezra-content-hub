[AM] Isaiah 6:3: Holy Is Yahweh of Armies

Good morning. Today is Tuesday, November 10th. This is Daily Devotion. The week has only reached its second day, and the things at hand are already lined up one after another: work to hand in, messages to answer, family to worry about. Before going out, first lift your eyes from these things for a little while, and hear how heaven calls the One we believe in.

Today's verse is Isaiah chapter 6, verse 3. The seraphim called to one another and said: Holy, holy, holy, is Yahweh of Armies! The whole earth is full of his glory!

Speaking of God's holiness, Tozer said that holiness is not one quality among the many God has; He Himself is holiness. When we measure holiness with a human ruler, the whitest white, brought before Him, is only a dim gray. That is why Moses had to take off his shoes before the burning bush, and why Isaiah, as soon as he heard this cry, said that his lips were unclean — woe is me, I am undone. Yet wonderfully, this holy God did not push people away: He sent a seraph to cleanse Isaiah with a coal from the altar, and He called him to go and speak. It is the same today: coming before Him in the morning, first admit our limits and our failures; there is no need to hurry to make ourselves sound a little better. Since His glory fills the whole earth, the road we walk today and the doors we enter are also before Him. Going out with this reverence, we will do our work carefully, and our hearts will actually be at peace.

Let's pray together. Heavenly Father, You are holy, and Your glory fills the whole earth. Please cleanse our hearts, so that today we revere You and draw near to You, and do the work in our hands honestly and carefully. In Jesus' name, Amen.

May the Lord let you today, in His holiness, have both reverence and peace. See you tomorrow.
