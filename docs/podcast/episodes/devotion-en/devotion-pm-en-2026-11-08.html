[PM] My Soul Is Stilled Like a Weaned Child

一句话要点：诗篇131:2：心平稳安静如婴孩——诗篇 131:2 的话陪你走今天这一步，讲解与祷告均为原创。

逐字稿全文

Good evening. Today is Sunday, November 8th. A busy weekend has come to tonight: the body is tired, but the heart may still be tense — tomorrow is a new week again, and the things to worry about are already lining up at the door. Tonight before sleep, hear David use the softest of pictures to describe the steadiest of hearts — like a child, leaning in its mother's arms.

Tonight's verse is Psalm 131, verse 2. The Scripture says: Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul, like a weaned child with his mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me.

A weaned child lying in its mother's arms is no longer there for milk; it is there simply to lean — He is there, and that is enough to rest. David says his heart is like that: it is not that there are no storms; it is that he has learned not to fuss. Psalm 131 is very short; in the verse before, he says that matters too great and too unsearchable are not for him to grasp at. Some things cannot be worked out tonight, and some of tomorrow is still unknown. David's way is not to force an answer, but to put his heart back into those arms. Tonight, set those unsearchable things down for a while as well: if He is the One who holds us, our part is not to calculate all night, but first to be still, to lean on Him, and to sleep. A child does not calculate tomorrow; a child confirms only one thing: whether He is there. You are there, and he sleeps. Once the heart is stilled, tomorrow's things can be entrusted tomorrow, one by one.

Let's pray together. Lord, thank You that You hold us as a mother holds her child. Tonight, still and quiet our hearts; let us not be disturbed by tomorrow's things, but sleep in peace in Your arms. In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

Good night. See you tomorrow.
